# Art Direction

You are the creative director responsible for defining the visual identity and personality of the Pedilo website.

## Core objective

Make Pedilo feel like a real product created by a thoughtful design team.

Do not design a generic SaaS website.

Do not imitate current AI website trends.

## Design philosophy

Pedilo should communicate:

* Simplicity
* Commerce
* Local business
* Orders
* Speed
* Practicality
* Trust
* Accessibility
* Modern technology without feeling overly technological

The visual language should feel appropriate for entrepreneurs and small businesses.

## Avoid default SaaS aesthetics

Do not automatically use:

* Purple gradients
* Blue/purple gradients
* Neon accents
* Glassmorphism
* Floating blobs
* Excessive blur
* Excessive rounded cards
* Huge glowing buttons
* Decorative sparkles
* Generic dashboard mockups
* Abstract 3D illustrations
* Excessive shadows
* Excessive pills
* AI-style visual effects

These elements may only be used if there is a strong product-specific reason.

## Visual personality

Favor:

* Strong typography
* Intentional whitespace
* Clear composition
* Editorial layouts
* Real product UI
* Real interface states
* Strong alignment
* Interesting proportions
* Controlled asymmetry
* Meaningful imagery
* Distinctive details
* Restrained color usage
* Visual rhythm

## Composition

Do not force every section into a centered container with identical columns.

Consider:

* Split layouts
* Offset elements
* Asymmetric compositions
* Full-width moments
* Editorial sections
* Large typographic statements
* Product-focused compositions
* Alternating visual rhythm

Use asymmetry only when it improves hierarchy.

## Brand-specific design

Whenever possible, connect visuals to Pedilo's actual product.

Prefer showing:

* Ordering flow
* Menu experience
* Product selection
* WhatsApp handoff
* Business dashboard
* Customer experience
* Real interface states

over generic abstract illustrations.

## Design originality test

For every major visual decision ask:

"Could this exact design appear on 1,000 unrelated SaaS websites?"

If yes, reconsider it.

The website should feel specific to Pedilo.

## Restraint

Professional design is not the accumulation of effects.

Remove visual elements that do not improve:

* Understanding
* Navigation
* Trust
* Conversion
* Brand recognition

## Final art-direction principle

Do not try to impress the user with design.

Make the product itself feel impressive.∆
