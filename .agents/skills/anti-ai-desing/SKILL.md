# Anti-AI Design

You are responsible for detecting and eliminating visual patterns commonly associated with generic AI-generated websites.

## Primary objective

The website must look intentionally designed, not generated from a generic prompt.

## Never use a visual trend merely because it is popular.

Every major design decision must have a clear purpose.

## Avoid these patterns by default

### Layout

Avoid:

* Generic centered hero
* Hero + floating dashboard + gradient background
* Repeated three-card sections
* Repeated alternating image/text sections
* Excessive card grids
* Every section inside a rounded container
* Identical section structures
* Excessive centered text

### Visual effects

Avoid:

* Purple/blue gradients
* Neon glows
* Excessive blur
* Glassmorphism
* Background blobs
* Decorative noise without purpose
* Excessive shadows
* Excessive borders 
* Gradient text
* Glowing CTA buttons

### Components

Avoid:

* Excessive pills
* Excessive rounded corners
* Decorative icons everywhere
* Cards used for every piece of information
* Unnecessary badges
* Excessive floating UI

### Motion

Avoid:

* Every element fading in
* Every section sliding in
* Constant parallax
* Excessive hover effects
* Decorative animation

### Copy

Avoid generic phrases such as:

* "Revolutionize your business"
* "Take your business to the next level"
* "The future of..."
* "All-in-one platform"
* "Powerful solution"
* "Seamlessly"
* "Transform your business"
* "Unlock your potential"

unless the wording genuinely fits the product and context.

## AI smell test

Before approving a design ask:

1. Does this look like a template?
2. Could another SaaS use the same design?
3. Is the decoration doing anything?
4. Is the visual hierarchy intentional?
5. Does the product itself appear?
6. Does the page have a recognizable personality?
7. Would a professional designer defend each visual decision?

If the answer is no, redesign.

## Human design characteristics

Favor:

* Intentional imperfections
* Strong typographic choices
* Real product imagery
* Specific visual metaphors
* Meaningful composition
* Controlled asymmetry
* Distinctive details
* Strong hierarchy
* Fewer but better components
* Clear visual rhythm

## Important

Do not replace generic AI design with generic minimalist design.

"Minimalist" is not a design direction.

The objective is distinctive, intentional design.
